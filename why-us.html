Our identity

Why us

Mission, vision, and values that guide our teaching and community.

Mission

To provide excellence in Spanish language teaching, enhancing our educational experience with new technologies and resources. Our purpose is to foster curiosity, enthusiasm, and autonomy in our students, creating opportunities that contribute to their personal development within the community and in harmony with the environment.

Vision

To be an inspiring and innovative educational space that promotes Spanish learning as a tool capable of transforming lives and opening doors to the world.

Values

  • Cooperation
  • Respect
  • Sustainability
  • Quality
